# Vanti (Vanti Analytics)

> Vanti Analytics was an adaptive-AI-for-manufacturing company (defect detection, early fault prediction, predictive quality, process tuning) backed by Insight Partners. As of 2026-07-21 the company has no live web or API presence: vanti-analytics.com has lapsed and is parked by a third party, vanti.ai is for sale, and the former app/support subdomains no longer resolve.
